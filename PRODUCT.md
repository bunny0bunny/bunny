# مكتبة بانيورا

An Arabic-first web library for serialized manga, manhwa, novels and comics. Anime is excluded. Mobile discovery and comfortable reading are the founder's primary priorities. Readers can start as guests and resume their progress; accounts provide the existing personal-library and community functionality.

Publishing is team-only. Author/translator credits do not confer publishing authority. Assigned contributors prepare work; administration controls public metadata and publishing. Preserve the C1 authorization foundation.

Build on v3/banny. v4 is context/sample material, not a replacement application or an automatic content import. Payments, downloads, open publishing and native apps are outside this implementation package. Existing demo material is illustrative; no fictional ratings, metadata or reading progress from design mockups may be inserted as real user data.

Content category and reading layout are separate: manga/manhwa/novel/comic versus manga-page/webtoon-scroll/novel-text routes. Existing URLs and saved progress must survive the redesign. Manhwa is explicitly confirmed by the founder.

Approved direction: content-first discovery A with genuine editorial recommendations B. Approved visual reference boards: home, explore, series detail and reader in DESIGN.md. The founder authorized implementation after viewing the complete design package. Review once at the package checkpoint; sensitive behavior needs targeted checks during work. Do not ask for repeated authorization for this approved scope.
